<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 10.3 is the consumer-quality follow-up after Phase 10.2.

Its job is not to reopen scope, fresh-news serving, or import/release promotion. Its job is to make the current `release_gate_required` subset consumable at the chunk layer:

- remove standalone `source_only` chunks such as `（来源：教务处）`
- remove standalone `terminal_boilerplate` chunks such as newsroom disclaimers and submission tails
- normalize promotable official-web chunk headers so they keep the labeled `标题：... | 章节：...` contract instead of drifting to unlabeled `compact_identity`
- make support-only roles explicit enough that retrieval and answer composition can ignore them if they ever reappear
- add machine-checkable audit truth for these residual failure modes

### In Scope

- `release_gate_required` only
- official-web article line classification and projection unit shaping
- official-web chunk header normalization for promotable content
- official-web support-only unit suppression in chunk/retrieval/answer paths
- official-web chunking audit truth for `source_only`, `terminal_boilerplate`, and header drift
- fixed-scope replay/QC closeout

### Out Of Scope

- `retain_inventory_only`
- `future_fresh_news_surface`
- fresh-news implementation or query enablement
- import/release candidate rerun
- crawl expansion, host expansion, scheduler promotion

### Root Cause

Phase 10.2 repaired paragraph over-aggregation, but replay sampling still shows three residual defects:

- `terminal_boilerplate_chunk`
  - `校长强世功接受“统战新语”专访 解读民族团结进步促进法`
  - still emits a disclaimer-only chunk containing `② 凡本网注明其他来源的作品...` / `③ ...请与我们联系。`
- `source_only_chunk`
  - `本科培养`
  - `博士后`
  - still emit standalone `（来源：...）` chunks
- `header_context_drift`
  - `学校概况` short chunk falls back to `学校概况 / 学校简介`
  - `“民图一小时”信息素养讲座预告` short chunks fall back to `title / section` compact identity instead of labeled headers

The remaining problem is no longer unit granularity. It is that official-web projection/chunking still lacks explicit support-only role handling and still lets short article chunks optimize away the labeled context contract.

### Success Shape

- projection explicitly distinguishes `notice_fact` / `reference_fact` from `source_attribution` / `boilerplate`
- support-only official-web units do not become retrieval chunks
- promotable official-web chunks keep labeled headers whenever section context exists
- chunking audit can explicitly surface support-only chunks and header drift if they regress
- focused tests, replay, and sampling align to the repaired current gate
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Locked by current repo truth

- Only `release_gate_required` participates in the current answer-quality gate.
- `retain_inventory_only` and `future_fresh_news_surface` remain explicit non-gate boundaries.
- Phase 10 freshness fail-close behavior stays untouched.
- This phase improves chunk-consumption quality; it does not promote the repaired replay into a new release by itself.

### Locked by current cleaning/chunking policy

- `article_detail` chunk intent remains “one section or 1-3 continuous paragraphs under one section path”.
- Promotable official-web chunks must preserve labeled page/section context, not unlabeled `compact_identity`.
- Listing pages remain audit-only and are not the target of this phase.

### The agent's discretion

- the exact article-line classification helper and buffering shape
- which support-only roles are skipped from chunk building versus retained only in structured-unit truth
- the exact audit issue-code names and example payloads
</decisions>
