# Phase 04.2: Cloudflare Runtime Topology And Delivery Shape - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-03-25
**Status:** Ready for planning
**Source:** Derived from the inserted roadmap phase, Phase 04.1 architecture outputs, and current planning/state constraints

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Phase 04.2 freezes the Cloudflare-to-runtime delivery topology for the already-approved two-surface model. The scope is to make the runtime-facing deployment shape explicit for:

- public entry in front of `apps/supervisor-api`
- protected internal panel as a future `apps/` runtime shell
- origin ownership, hostname routing, environment/config boundary, and rollback seam

This phase is not about implementing deployment code, selecting a final framework, or changing query-path semantics. It exists to make the delivery shape explicit while the mainline RAG path is blocked by handbook chunk-truth stabilization.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Locked by roadmap and Phase 04.1 outputs
- Cloudflare remains the edge/control boundary in front of repo-owned runtime shells.
- The system remains a two-surface model: one public entry surface and one protected internal panel surface.
- `apps/*` remain runtime/transport boundaries; `packages/*` remain stable capability boundaries.
- `apps/supervisor-api` remains the repo-owned runtime shell for the public entry path.
- Any future internal panel remains an `apps/` concern and must consume runtime APIs only.

### Locked phase intent for 04.2
- The topology between Cloudflare, origin runtime, public hostname, protected hostname, and rollback seam must be explicit.
- Delivery ownership must stay at the runtime boundary and must not leak Cloudflare/provider types into `packages/contracts`, `packages/supervisor-core`, `packages/rag-specialist`, or retrieval/data-plane packages.
- Secret/config ownership, environment split, and origin routing must be documented as deployment/runtime seams, not as package-level design changes.

### Explicit non-goals
- Do not decide `SupervisorRequest.actor` mapping.
- Do not solve retrieval ACL or audit/trace ownership.
- Do not choose a long-term runtime/framework beyond what Phase 04.1 already left open.
- Do not implement Cloudflare config, IaC, CI/CD, or containerization in this phase.
- Do not define panel business workflows or protected operator actions.

### The agent's Discretion
- Exact decomposition of the phase into one or more design artifacts, as long as they keep the topology, ownership, and rollback boundaries explicit.
- Whether to express the topology as one canonical architecture note plus one concise ops-oriented handoff, or a similarly small artifact set.
- The exact terminology for origin, runtime shell, edge route, and rollback unit, as long as it stays provider-aware at the edge and provider-neutral in core packages.

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Treat 04.2 as a delivery-topology freeze, not as a platform-selection or IaC implementation phase.
- Make origin routing, hostname ownership, secret/config boundary, and rollback scope explicit enough that 04.3 and 04.4 can plan against them without reopening 04.1.
- Keep the public entry origin centered on `apps/supervisor-api` and the panel origin centered on a future `apps/` shell, even if they later share some hosting substrate.

</specifics>
