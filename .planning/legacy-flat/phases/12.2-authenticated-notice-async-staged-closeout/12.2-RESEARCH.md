# Phase 12.2 Research: Async Staged Closeout

## Motivation
- Current closeout model repeats large cumulative imports; `run-14` just spent ~1h+ on the `1000` checkpoint alone.
- Source-truth contract says notice artifacts are immutable; document scope refresh is the risky single-writer stage.
- To scale, we must make expensive work (artifact writes, raw asset uploads, document-unit generation) asynchronous per shard while keeping scope refresh deterministic.

## Key Investigations
1. **Operations prefix**: need canonical storage under `campus_notices/operations/authenticated-notice-closeout/{run_id}/...` for frozen input, shard plan, milestone summaries, heartbeat logs, reconciliation snapshots.
2. **Staging tables**: design schema that records per run/per shard notices, raw assets, document artifacts, and their readiness state before promotion.
3. **Async shard executor**: figure out safe concurrency model (asyncio + aiobotocore/psycopg), zero-byte S3 writes, and raw asset upsert dedupe.
4. **Single promoter commit**: ensure final document scope update happens once after all shards succeed; staging tables feed a deterministic `replace_scope`.
5. **Verification**: deterministic check that staged artifacts match expected counts/digests, replacing `final rerun`.
